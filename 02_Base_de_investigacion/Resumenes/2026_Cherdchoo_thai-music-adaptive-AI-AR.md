## Resumen (del articulo)

. This paper proposes THAI-MUSE (Thai Music Understanding and Skill Enhancement), an innovative adaptive learning system for Thai traditional music education that integrates artiﬁcial intelligence and augmented reality technologies. The system addresses critical challenges in traditional music instruction, including limited access to expert teachers and declining student engagement. THAI-MUSE employs a novel approach combining deep learning for performance analysis with reinforcement learning for personalized content delivery, all surfaced through an augmented reality interface that provides real-time feedback. The proposed architecture features specialized neural network models for Thai musical elements and a culturally sensitive content adaptation algorithm. This paper presents the theoretical framework, system architecture, and technical speciﬁcations without implementation, focusing on how AI-driven adaptive learning can support the preservation and transmission of traditional musical knowledge while respecting cultural authenticity.
